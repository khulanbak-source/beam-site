Who it's for

Sales teams that meet clients all day and log it at night.

Sales reps

  • Logging feels like a second job. After a day of meetings, CRM fields are the last thing anyone wants to fill in.
  • Details fade. By the time a rep sits down to log, half the conversation is gone.
  • The CRM lives on a laptop. Reps live on their phones and in chat apps.

Sales managers

  • Chasing updates. "Did you log this?" comes up every single day.
  • A pipeline you can't trust. Patchy records hide which channels and approaches convert.
  • Hot leads go cold when a follow-up has no date and no owner.
How it works

Four steps your reps already know how to do.

01

Rep sends a message

A voice note or text in Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack or Teams, right after the meeting.

02

Beam pulls out the details

Name, company, stage, interest and next step. If something is missing, Beam asks once.

03

The CRM updates itself

New leads are created and returning ones updated. The rep confirms before anything is saved.

04

Follow-ups stay on track

Beam puts the follow-up in the calendar and sends each rep a daily list.

How we build it together

About two weeks from first call to handover.

Your team spends 6 to 8 hours in total. We do the rest between sessions.

  1. Week 1

    Discovery call

    We map your sales process, your CRM fields and how reps report today.

    1 hour together
  2. Week 1

    Accounts ready

    You open or share the chat app, AI key, CRM and n8n access using our checklist.

    30 min, your admin
  3. Week 1

    Build session

    We connect everything live with your team and log your first real update.

    3 to 4 hours together
  4. Week 2

    Pilot

    2 or 3 reps use it for real for a week. We tune the questions and fields.

    Normal work
  5. Week 2

    Handover

    Your admin learns to change fields and questions. You get a written guide and a recorded walkthrough.

    1 hour together

WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, Salesforce, or separate views for several teams take 3 to 5 weeks. Meta, Microsoft or Salesforce admins need to approve access, and there are more fields and roles to set up.

Questions

What teams ask before they book.

What if we need changes later?

Your admin can change fields and questions in n8n. For bigger additions, book another session.

Who owns the data?

You do. Records live in your CRM and your accounts, and access for our build is removed at handover.

Which languages does it understand?

Any language your AI model handles, including mixed Mongolian and English. For voice, we pick a speech model trained on your language.

Do we need a developer?

An admin comfortable with software settings is enough. We train them at handover.

Can we build it ourselves?

Yes. Our video tutorial walks your team through the main steps. Expect one team member to spend about 60 hours learning n8n, connecting the APIs and tuning the questions, spread over 6 to 8 weeks alongside their job.
